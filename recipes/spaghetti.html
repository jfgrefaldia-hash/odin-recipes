Spaghetti

Spaghetti is a classic Italian dish that is loved by many. It consists of long, thin pasta noodles served with a variety of sauces, such as marinara, Alfredo, or Bolognese. Spaghetti can be topped with grated Parmesan cheese and fresh herbs for added flavor.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb spaghetti
  • 1 jar of marinara sauce
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 onion, diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Grated Parmesan cheese for serving

Instructions

  1. Cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions. Drain and set aside.
  2. In a large skillet, cook the ground beef over medium heat until browned. Add the diced onion and minced garlic, and cook until the onion is translucent.
  3. Add the marinara sauce to the skillet and stir to combine. Simmer for 10-15 minutes, allowing the flavors to meld together. Season with salt and pepper to taste.
  4. Serve the cooked spaghetti topped with the meat sauce and a sprinkle of grated Parmesan cheese.
