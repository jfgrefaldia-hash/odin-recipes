Chocolate Chip Cookies

Ingredients

  • 1 cup butter softened
  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 1 cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 teaspoons vanila extract
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda
  • 2 teaspoons hot water
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 3 cups all all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups semisweet chocolate chips
  • 1 cup chopped walnuts

Instructions

  1. Gather your ingredients, making sure your butter is softened, and your eggs are room temperature.
  2. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C). Beat butter, white sugar, and brown sugar together in a large bowl with an electric mixer until light and creamy, about 4 minutes.
  3. Beat in eggs, one at a time, then stir in vanilla.
  4. Dissolve baking soda in hot water; add to batter along with salt and mix until combined.
  5. Stir in flour just until no dry steaks remain. Fold in chocolte chips and walnuts, if using, until evenly distributed.
  6. Drop rounded 1 1/2 tablespoons portion of dough 2 inches apart onto ungreased baking sheets, using a medium cookie scoop if desired.
  7. Bake in the preheated oven until the edges are lightly browned and the centers are just set, about 10 minutes.
  8. Lets the cookies cool briefly on the baking sheets, then transfer them to a wire rack to cool completely.
  9. Serve or store cooled cookies in an airtight container.
