Smores Brownies

Smores brownies are a delicious dessert that combines the rich, fudgy texture of brownies with the classic flavors of s'mores. These brownies are topped with a layer of marshmallows and graham cracker crumbs, creating a gooey and crunchy treat that is perfect for any occasion.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 1/2 cup butter, softened
  • 3/4 cup granulated sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 cup marshmallows
  • 1 cup graham cracker crumbs

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350 degrees F and line an 8x8 pan with parchment paper.
  2. Whisk together the flour, cocoa powder, salt, and baking powder in a medium bowl.
  3. In a large bowl, beat the softened butter and sugar until light and fluffy, then mix in the eggs one at a time followed by the vanilla.
  4. Stir the dry ingredients into the wet mixture until just combined, dont overmix.
  5. Spread half the batter into the pan, scatter the marshmallows evenly on top, then dollop the remaining batter over the marshmallows and gently spread it out.
  6. Sprinkle the graham cracker crumbs evenly over the top.
  7. Bake for 25-30 minutes, or until a toothpick insterted in the center comes out with a few moist crumbs.
  8. Let the brownies cool completely in the pan before slicing.
